Common questions

How do I choose the right paving?

It depends on your home, how you use the space and your budget. When we visit we talk you through the colours, finishes and patterns that suit your property so you can choose with confidence.

Can you match the patio to my driveway?

Yes. We build driveways, paths and patios, so we can plan them together and make them look like they belong to the same property.

What guarantees do you give?

Full workmanship guarantees are provided with every installation, as well as guarantees from the product manufacturers.
