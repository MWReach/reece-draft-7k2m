Marshalls awards

We are Marshalls regional and national award winners, including national winners of Best Driveway 2010 in the Marshalls Register Awards. The awards give contractors the chance to be recognised by fellow professionals, and projects are presented anonymously.

The Marshalls Register of Approved Landscape Contractors and Driveway Installers is a national network of independent professional installers. They are not employed by Marshalls, but are vetted and monitored to assess their competence and performance.

Bradstone Assured Awards

We have won Best Large Driveway Transformation twice at the national Bradstone Assured Awards.

Reece Paving & Driveways Ltd is a truly worthy winner of the Bradstone Assured award. Their ability to provide an outstanding standard of landscaping and dedicated customer service was unrivalled in this category. We at Bradstone are proud to have them as a member of our trusted scheme of installers, and we wish them continued success for the future.Peter Montgomery, National Sales Manager, Bradstone

Bradstone Assured is a national network of professional installers carefully selected and approved by Bradstone for the levels of quality and craftsmanship they provide. To join, installers must meet strict selection criteria, so customers have peace of mind that their project will be completed to a high professional standard.
