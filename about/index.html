About Reece Landscapes

Improving your environment, one driveway, patio and garden at a time.

Meet Dan

A total commitment to quality and service

I strive to keep my designs and work as fresh and as unique as possible by using a mixture of new and current materials, and by adding my own twist. My aim is to provide every customer with a total commitment to quality and service, and to improving your environment.

I am very proud to have been recognised by Marshalls and to have achieved this high status within my industry, and I believe it is reflected in my work. Professionalism and careful attention to detail are a given on every project.

Reece Landscapes has been delivering bespoke driveways, landscaped gardens and patios for over 30 years across Cheshire, Manchester and the North West.

Standards

Assessed on every project

As a member of the Marshalls Register of approved contractors, and as Tobermore approved and Bradstone Assured landscapers, we abide by a strict code of practice. We are assessed on every project, so your job and our craftsmanship are carried out to a high standard.
