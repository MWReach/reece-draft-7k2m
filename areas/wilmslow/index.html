We cover Wilmslow and the surrounding area.

Reece Landscapes is a family run landscaping business based in Poynton, Cheshire. We have over 30 years of experience building driveways, patios and gardens, and we are Marshalls regional and national award winners, Marshalls Register approved, Bradstone Assured and Tobermore approved.

If you are in Wilmslow and thinking about a new block paved driveway, a patio or a garden makeover, we will visit your property, talk through your ideas and give you a free quote. The 10 year guarantee applies to Marshalls, Tobermore and Bradstone installations, and we explain what covers your job before you decide.

Before you start

Paving over a front garden can need planning permission, depending on the size and the type of surface, and a dropped kerb needs separate permission. Your local authority for Wilmslow is Cheshire East Council, and we are happy to explain what usually applies when we visit.

Do you cover Wilmslow?

Is a free quote available in Wilmslow?

Do I need permission from Cheshire East Council?

It depends on your project. Paving a front garden with a surface that does not drain can need planning permission, and a dropped kerb needs separate permission from the highways authority. We will explain what usually applies, and you can always check with Cheshire East Council.
