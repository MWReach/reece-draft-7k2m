Privacy notice

How we use the details you give us.

Last updated 2026-11-18.

Who we are

Reece Paving & Driveways Ltd (trading as Reece Landscapes) is the controller of your personal information. Contact us at info@reecelandscapes.co.uk or on 07730 512061. Address: 59 Micawber Road, Poynton, Cheshire SK12 1UP.

What we collect

If you use the enquiry form, we collect your name, phone number, email address (optional) and the message you write. If you call, email or message us on WhatsApp, we hold the details you give us in that conversation.

Why we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, arrange a visit and provide a quote, and to carry out any work you ask us to do. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering a contract, and our legitimate interest in running our business.

Who sees it

Enquiry form messages are sent to us by email through a form service called Web3Forms, which processes them on our behalf. We do not sell your details or use them for marketing you have not asked for.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any work that follows, and for a reasonable period afterwards for our business records and legal obligations.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us and we will respond. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
