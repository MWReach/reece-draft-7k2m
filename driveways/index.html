Common questions

Do I need planning permission for a new driveway?

Not always. Rules in England say that paving a front garden with a surface that does not let rainwater drain away needs planning permission if the area is bigger than 5 square metres, unless the water drains to a lawn or border or you use a permeable surface. If you need a dropped kerb to cross the pavement, that is agreed separately with the local highways authority. Ask us when you get in touch and we will explain what applies to your property, and check with your council if you are unsure.

What guarantees do you give?

The 10 year guarantee applies to Marshalls, Tobermore and Bradstone installations. We also use other trusted suppliers, so ask us what guarantee covers the materials you choose. We explain it all before you decide.
